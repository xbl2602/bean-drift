Where on the Moon do you want to stand?

Pick a landing site. You stand there at eye height (1.6 m) on real terrain, and the Sun, the Earth and the stars move on a real ephemeris: drag the time slider to run a day, a month or a year.

选一个着陆点，以真人视角（1.6 米）站在真实地形上；拖动时间条，太阳、地球和星空按真实星历运动。

Terrain: NASA LOLA and LROC NAC. Sun, Earth and stars: built-in ephemeris, checked against JPL Horizons. Rendering engine comparison (technical test)
